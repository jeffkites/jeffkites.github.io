Wind ranges and rider levels are the credited author’s guidance. Check current local access, wind, tides and conditions. Weather gauges are reference stations, not readings at each launch.

About the readings

These are readings at individual gauges. Wind can differ across the lake, around headlands and along beaches. Compass labels describe where the wind comes from; arrows point in the direction it blows. The map does not estimate wind between stations.

BOM histories refresh from their published observations. Marks Point’s public readings do not include a measurement timestamp in this view. Salts Bay / Swansea Heads is one Marine Rescue station. Refresh checks its public readings and chart data; if the source declines the request, its original chart remains available. Nelson Bay reads the Marine Rescue station’s published wind observations and history via WillyWeather.
